# Uber Eats Business Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Uber Eats Business Analytics Dashboard provides a visual overview of business performance across different cities and states in India. It focuses on analyzing revenue distribution, order patterns and geographical performance through interactive charts and visualizations.

The dashboard helps understand which cities and states contribute the most to revenue and how order volume relates to overall revenue.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue generated across different cities and states.
- Identify top-performing locations based on revenue.
- Visualize the geographical distribution of orders across India.
- Understand the relationship between order volume and revenue.
- Present business data in a clear and easy-to-understand format.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Dataset:** Uber Eats Sales / Orders Dataset
- **Charts:** Bar Chart, Pie Chart, Scatter Plot and Map Visualization

## 📊 Dashboard Features

**1. Revenue by City**
- Displays revenue generated by individual cities.
- Helps identify high-performing and low-performing cities.
- Makes city-wise revenue comparison easier.

**2. Revenue by State**
- Compares revenue contribution across different Indian states.
- Highlights states generating the highest revenue.

**3. Orders on Map**
- Provides a geographical representation of orders across India.
- Helps understand the distribution of business activity across different locations.

**4. Revenue Pie Chart**
- Shows the distribution of revenue across different categories.
- Provides a quick view of each category's contribution.

**5. Orders vs Revenue**
- Uses a scatter plot to compare the number of orders against revenue.
- Helps identify the relationship between sales volume and revenue generation.


## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Compare revenue performance across multiple cities.
- Identify states with higher revenue contributions.
- Understand geographical patterns in order distribution.
- Analyze how order volume is associated with revenue.
- Identify locations that may have opportunities for business growth.
